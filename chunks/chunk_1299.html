Retell AI: production voice-agent evidence map

Retell AI positions itself as an AI Voice Agent Platform for Phone Call Centers. First-party surfaces cover phone agents, APIs, webhooks, knowledge bases, simulation, batch calling, and enterprise controls.

Five non-overlapping wedges

  1. Phone-call-first production voice agents.
  2. Simulation and testing.
  3. Batch outbound calling.
  4. Knowledge-base plus webhook/API orchestration.
  5. YC-linked developer platform.
