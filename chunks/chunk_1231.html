Evidence questions buyers will ask

  1. Can one failed evaluation join to the exact task, agent version, capability, scorer, metric version, environment, and result?
  2. Can a deployed agent be traced back to its model, policy revision, training run, optimization run, and release decision?
  3. Can a red-team trajectory be reproduced from sandbox or World through tool calls, traces, observations, remediation, and human override?
  4. Can on-prem boundaries, retention, deletion, residency, and cross-tenant isolation be demonstrated for every run?
  5. Can the platform export one control receipt usable for procurement, incident review, model-risk review, and AI-governance audit?
