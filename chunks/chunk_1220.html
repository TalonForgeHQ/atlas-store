The five highest-priority evidence gaps

  1. Trace-to-evaluation edge: every production trace needs the exact dataset, evaluator, and online-evaluation result that judged it.
  2. Production version join: the trace must point to the prompt, model, tool, and code revisions that were live at execution time.
  3. Quality–cost–latency triad: quality decisions must be replayable alongside the cost and latency signals that affected release or rollback decisions.
  4. Deployment-to-rollback chain: a failed evaluation should join to remediation, re-evaluation, release, deployment, and rollback evidence without a manual dashboard search.
  5. Retention and isolation: the export must prove retention, deletion, regional processing, and cross-tenant boundaries for traces, datasets, feedback, and attachments.
