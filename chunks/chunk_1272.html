  5. Distribution primitive (Vercel-native + Google-native + Arize-native + Replit-native): v0 ships a Vercel-native deploy lane (one-click deploy to Vercel/Next.js). Stitch ships a Google-native design lane (Figma + Vertex AI integration). Visual Electric ships a designer-native canvas (no vendor lock-in).
