The five highest-priority evidence gaps

  1. Evaluation-to-outcome edge: every production outcome needs the exact evaluation, dataset or golden-set version, metric, judge, and score that assessed it.
  2. Reliability-version join: the replay must preserve the prompt, model, tool, evaluator, and threshold versions active at execution time.
  3. Observe-to-protect chain: a monitoring or hallucination finding should join to the protection policy, allow/block/escalate decision, and human-review record.
  4. Remediation-to-release chain: a failed evaluation or protection decision should join to remediation, re-evaluation, deployment, and rollback evidence without manual dashboard searches.
  5. Retention and isolation: the export must prove retention, deletion, regional processing, and cross-tenant boundaries for traces, evaluation records, prompts, and attachments.
