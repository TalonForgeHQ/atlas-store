The 5 evidence gaps behind one Evisort AI redline

  1. Dataset version drift: can your auditor replay which dataset_version produced the redline proposal at the time of the redline? If the dataset is updated after the redline, the trail loses the ground-truth provenance.
  2. Clause → playbook join: can your trail join the redline proposal back to the exact clause_id, clause_version, and playbook_id that produced the diff? Most CLM trails snapshot the document but not the rule.
  3. Approver chain provenance: does the trail capture the approver, the approval step, the human override, and the signature event as one replayable chain, or as separate audit tables that have to be re-joined at audit time?
  4. Obligation extraction seam: does the redline receipt end at signature, or does it continue into the obligation-extraction product so the auditor can see which obligations were derived from the redline clause?
  5. Workday tenant boundary: with Workday CLM, does the trail join the Workday tenant id, the Evisort tenant id, and the contract id so cross-tenant no-bleed invariants can be checked at audit time?
