## Why Trustible is the next governance sibling

Trustible's first-party platform describes one continuous AI-governance lifecycle: Intake, Manage Risk, Monitor, and Comply. Structured intake captures AI use cases before production and automatically scores and routes them by risk tier. Risk management adds assessments and orchestrated reviews. Monitoring preserves post-approval oversight through scheduled reviews and portfolio visibility. Compliance maps controls across frameworks and assembles audit-ready evidence from real governance activity.

Trustible is SIBLING #4/5 because it adds the cohort's workflow-native governance-operations lane.
